## Objective

Build a high-performance, premium boutique web studio site for **Marquee Social**. The site itself must be the strongest proof of capability — converting visitors into proposal requests and bookings.

## Design Direction

- **Typography:** Bold, large type with strong visual hierarchy
- **Color:** Dark or high-contrast base with one precise accent colour (matching the black & silver logo)
- **Storytelling:** Portfolio-first — show work before explaining services
- **Motion:** Subtle, refined scroll-triggered reveals and smooth transitions
- **Feel:** Premium and precise — not noisy, not "agency cliché"
- **Mobile-first**, extremely fast load times, frictionless booking
- **CTAs everywhere:** WhatsApp click-to-chat and click-to-call on every page
